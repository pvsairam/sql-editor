## Work with results

- Click, drag, **Shift**/**Ctrl**-click to select cells, rows (row number) or columns (Ctrl+click header).
- **Ctrl+C** copies; **Ctrl+Shift+C** adds headers. *Copy* menu: CSV, JSON, Markdown, INSERT statements, `IN (...)` list.
- Double-click a cell to see its full value (JSON/XML are pretty-printed).
- Sort by header, filter with the box (**Ctrl+F**) or per-column filters (`>100`, `!x`, `empty`).
- **Export** to Excel, CSV or JSON. **Fetch more** loads the next batch from the server.
